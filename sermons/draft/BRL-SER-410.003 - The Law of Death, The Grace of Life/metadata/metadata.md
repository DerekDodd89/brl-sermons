## Sermon Proposition

The Law reveals man's inability to obtain righteousness, but through the
self-sacrifice of Christ, God's grace provides the way for the sinner to
die with Christ and live unto God.

## Sermon Progression

**THE LAW → THE CRUCIFIED → THE GRACE**

## Main Points

1.  **Death Under the Law** --- Galatians 2:19
2.  **Death With the Lord** --- Galatians 2:20
3.  **Deliverance Through His Grace** --- Galatians 2:21

## Sermon Summary

Paul moves from the insufficiency of the Mosaic Law to the believer's
participation in the death of Christ and finally to the grace of God
manifested through Christ's self-sacrifice. The Law exposes sin and
condemns but cannot provide justification. In Christ, the old man is
crucified, the believer enters His death, and a new life begins in which
Christ lives in him. Paul concludes with a powerful Necessary Inference:
if righteousness could come through the Law, then Christ died in vain.

## Historical Focus

The Mosaic sacrificial system repeatedly demonstrated the seriousness of
sin and anticipated the sufficient sacrifice of Christ. Animal blood
could not itself provide final removal of sin; Christ fulfilled the
sacrificial anticipation through His self-offering.

## Doctrinal Focus

Justification cannot be obtained through Mosaic Law-keeping. God's grace
is decisively manifested in Christ loving us and giving Himself for us.
The believer dies with Christ and begins a new life unto God in which
Christ lives in him.

## Evangelistic Connection

Romans 6:3--6 connects participation in Christ's death with baptism into
His death. Galatians 3:27 confirms baptism into Christ within the same
epistle.
